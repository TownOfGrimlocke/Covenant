<Info>These discounts are limited to active role-players only</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How Do I Apply for My Discount?">
    You need to open a Support ticket on our Discord Server. If you are unable to open a ticket, please reach out to a member of Staff in-world.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the Discount Tiers?">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Businesses">
        10% discount available after 2 weeks of active role-play.
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Emergency Services & Education">
        15% discount available after 2 weeks of active role-play.

        <Info>This also applies to Teachers, CPS, & Hearth Staff.</Info>
      </Tab>

      <Tab title="Department Heads">
        20% discount available after 2 weeks of active role-play.     
        
        <Info>This Currently only applies to Fire, Medical, Sheriff, and Education Department Heads.</Info>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the Requirements?">
    You must be active within the community a min of 3 days a week or the discount is subject to removal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How Long Does It Take to Get Approved?">
    Once you have submitted your ticket, please allow up to 48 hours for a response. We will review your roleplay activity and notify you of the decision.
  </Accordion> 
</AccordionGroup>
