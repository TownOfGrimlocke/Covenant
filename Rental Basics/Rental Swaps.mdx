<Warning>
    Only the Renter, not subtenants, can request a transfer
</Warning> 

Rental Swaps will only occur on **ALL** of the following conditions: 

- A Minimum of 1 Week must be paid on the NEW rental before the transfer will be processed. 
-  Open a support ticket in our Discord or ask for a member of the rental team in the OOC group. 

<Info>
We push Discord only because it's the fastest way to get you assistance. 
</Info>
